1.19.4.1-5 — Lighter water reflection map, 1.19.4.1, descriptions in 9 languages

[b]Optimised[/b]
[list]
[*][b]2 MB less video memory.[/b] The water/river reflection map is pure black (that is what removes the sky reflection). It was still stored at the game's full 256×256 size with 9 mip levels on all six cube faces. A black cube map looks identical at any size, so it is now 4×4 per face: 2 MB → 632 bytes. Less VRAM and less texture bandwidth on every water and river pixel, with exactly the same image.
[/list]

[list]
[*]Rebuilt and verified against 1.19.4.1. None of the 165 game files this mod is built from changed in that patch.
[*]Tested in game on 1.19.4.1 with every DLC enabled: loads cleanly, water renders as before, no error-log entry mentions any file of this mod.
[*]Description now available in Turkish, German, French, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Russian, Polish, Simplified Chinese and Brazilian Portuguese. Steam shows it in your client language automatically.
[*]Source code, build tools and the full technical changelog are now public on GitHub (link in the description).
[/list]
